Before you open a file

Use an export you have reviewed, with living and unknown people, private notes, contact details and media removed. This beta displays ancestors with recorded deaths before 1926. Recently deceased people and uncertain records are withheld too.

The website is public, but your tree stays in this browser. Nothing is saved by the viewer. Clear it whenever you wish; refreshing or leaving the page clears it too. Access also expires after 30 minutes without activity or two hours.

The original file stays untouched. Notes, source prose, media and raw records are not shown. Automated filtering cannot reliably anonymise every name or place; review the export first.
